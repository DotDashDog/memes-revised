Are these two the same meme?

100 pairs of r/memes posts from 2019. Two yes/no questions per pair. Your answers are saved as you go and are compared against labels made by agents, which you are not shown. About 20 minutes; you can stop and come back.

What the two questions mean

Same template: both are built on the same base image or visual format, whatever the caption says. Two Drake "no / yes" posts are the same template.

Same meme by meaning: both are instances of the same specific joke, trend, event or catchphrase, so that seeing one you would say "that meme again", whatever the picture. Two "storm Area 51" jokes on different formats are the same meme. Two posts that only share a broad subject (school, gaming, Reddit) are not. Two posts on the same template with unrelated jokes are not.

Answer from your own judgment. Use "Unsure" when you could argue it either way.

Keys: 1 2 3 answer the template question (yes, no, unsure), q w e the meaning question, ← → move between pairs.
